What Arcane Ledger is

Arcane Ledger is a collection-tracking and deck-coaching companion for Magic: The Gathering players. It is an unofficial tool: it is not affiliated with, endorsed, sponsored, or approved by Wizards of the Coast LLC.

Prices and card data are estimates

Card prices, collection values, completion percentages, and "cost to finish" figures are estimates derived from third-party market data (via Scryfall for cards, and JustTCG for sealed products), may be inaccurate or out of date, and are not offers to buy or sell. Always verify prices with a retailer before any transaction. Scan results and card identifications can be wrong, so verify anything that matters.

AI advice is advice

Rue, the AI advisor, can make mistakes about rules, cards, and strategy. Its answers are suggestions, not official rulings. For tournament play, the judge is always right and Rue is not a judge.

Fair use of AI features

The AI features (Rue and the AI deck tools) carry reasonable daily limits so the service stays fast, affordable, and available to everyone. Curator includes a generous fair-use allowance (currently up to 100 Rue questions a day) and the free tier includes a smaller daily allowance. These limits may change over time. Automated, scripted, resold, or otherwise abusive use of the AI features may be rate-limited, paused, or restricted.

Subscriptions (Curator)

Curator is an optional subscription billed through your app store, meaning Google Play on Android and the App Store on iOS. New Curator subscriptions begin with a seven-day free trial; unless you cancel in your store's subscription settings before the trial ends, it converts to a paid subscription and the store charges the plan price. Free trials, renewals, price changes, and cancellation are all managed in that store's subscription settings. Cancel anytime there and you keep Curator until the end of the paid period. Your collection data is never deleted for unsubscribing.

Attic Pass (one-time purchase)

The Attic Pass is an optional one-time purchase, not a subscription. It never auto-renews and there is nothing to cancel. Each purchase unlocks the Attic Pass features (currently hands-free auto-scanning and full-detail import) for 30 days from that purchase. When the 30 days end, those features simply lock again and the app tells you so; your collection data is never affected. You can buy another pass whenever you like. A new purchase starts a fresh 30-day window from its own purchase date, including if you buy again before the current window ends. The pass follows your store account, so reinstalling or switching devices restores it through the store's normal restore flow. Billing and refunds for the Attic Pass are handled by your app store under its policies.
