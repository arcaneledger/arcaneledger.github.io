WHAT IT DOES

SCAN

Card Scanner

Point your camera at a card and it's in your ledger — instant on-device recognition, online or off. Hands-free auto-scan counts whole stacks, duplicates included, and can match the exact printing you own: borderless, alt-art, foils.

BUILD

Deck Brain

Your collection, measured against a living library of real archetypes across Commander, Modern, Pioneer, Legacy, and Pauper — kept current monthly from real tournament data. See every deck's completion %, what's missing, and what it costs to finish.

ASK

Mox — AI Deck Advisor

A rules-savvy MTG advisor grounded in real card text. Ask about interactions, get deck advice tuned to the cards you actually own, and settle table disputes fast.

TRACK

Collection Value

Live market pricing with history charts, gainers and losers, price alerts, and multi-store comparison — know what your binder is really worth.

PLAY

Playgroup & Game Table

Add friends, message your group, match trades, and get told the moment you and a friend can build a deck together tonight. Life counters, commander damage, real 3D dice and coin flips for game night.

FREE

Explorer & Mythic

Explorer is free forever — unlimited collection tracking and scanning. Mythic adds the intelligence: exact-printing scans, missing-card lists with cost-to-finish, unlimited Mox, and more.
