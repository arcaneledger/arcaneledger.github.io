WHAT IT DOES

SCAN

Card Scanner

Point your camera at a card and it's in your ledger — instant on-device recognition, online or off. Hands-free auto-scan counts whole stacks, duplicates included. Premium scans identify the exact printing you own on most cards — foils detected automatically — and a one-tap picker handles the rare miss.

BUILD

Deck Brain

Your collection, measured against a living library of real archetypes across Commander, Modern, Pioneer, Legacy, and Pauper — kept current monthly from real-world data: tournament decklists for the constructed formats, and EDHREC deck-building data for Commander. See every deck's completion %, what's missing, and what it costs to finish — then build it your way: the original printings, or the cheapest path as a real shopping list, with honest prices, never made-up numbers.

IMPORT

Bring Your Decks

Paste a Moxfield or Archidekt link — or a plain list — and your deck lands with the exact printings and finishes you chose, foils and all, not generic stand-ins. The original builder is always credited, and you can fork any import into a deck you're free to edit.

DISCOVER

Community Decks

Browse decks real players built and shared — filter by format, sort by what's popular, and find moderator-approved and Official picks. Submit your own in a tap; every shared deck credits its original creator.

ASK

Moxi — AI Deck Advisor

A rules-savvy MTG advisor grounded in real card text. Get deck advice tuned to the cards you actually own, and reason through tricky rules interactions — working from the real card text, and honest when it's not sure.

KNOW

Why a Card Matters

An honest read on any card: how it actually plays — the archetypes it defines and how often it sees play, from real deck data — kept deliberately separate from its price, so a pricey card is never mistaken for a powerful one.

ODDS

Deck Odds

Play the percentages: mathematically-proven draw odds, an opening-hand simulator, and a read on how fast a deck can close — turn a hunch into a number before game night.

TRACK

Collection Value

Live market pricing with history charts, gainers and losers, price alerts, and multi-store comparison — know what your binder is really worth.

PLAY

Playgroup & Game Table

Add friends, message your group, match trades, and get told the moment you and a friend can build a deck together tonight. Life counters, commander damage, dice and coin flips for game night.

PLANS

Explorer & Mythic

Explorer is free forever — unlimited collection tracking and scanning. Mythic is the optional paid upgrade that adds the intelligence: exact-printing scans, missing-card lists with cost-to-finish, the cheapest-path shopping list, honest card insights, unlimited Moxi, and New-Set Radar — what's on your wishlist and in your colors the moment a new set drops.

Plays in 10 languages — English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean & Chinese — online or off.

HONEST BY DESIGN

In a hobby full of hype, we'd rather tell you the truth — even when a flashier lie would look better.

  • Real prices only. The cheapest-build shopping list shows a cheaper printing only when one truly exists — we never invent a number to look helpful.
  • Playability kept separate from price. A card's cost never stands in for how good it is, so an expensive card is never dressed up as a powerful one.
  • Moxi won't bluff. The AI answers from real card text and tells you when it doesn't know, instead of making up a ruling.
  • The exact card you own. Scans and imports keep the real printing and finish — not generic stand-ins.
  • Your data, on your device. Scanning works on-device and offline; prices are clearly labeled as third-party estimates.
