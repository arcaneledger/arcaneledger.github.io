How it works

From shoebox to game night in three steps

1

Scan your cards

Point your camera at a card and it's in your binder — instant, on-device, free, and unlimited. It recognizes the exact printing you own on most cards: set, collector number, even foils.

2

See what you can build

Your collection is measured against a living library of real archetypes — kept current monthly from tournament results and Commander data. Every deck shows its completion % and exactly what's missing.

3

Get coached to the finish

Pick a deck and the app charts the cheapest real path to finish it — an honest shopping list with live prices, never invented numbers. Then track it, play it, and watch it grow.

Why players trust it

Honest by design

In a hobby full of hype, we'd rather tell you the truth —
even when a flashier lie would look better.

  • Real prices only. The cheapest-build list shows a cheaper printing only when one truly exists — we never invent a number to look helpful.
  • Playability kept separate from price. A card's cost never stands in for how good it is, so an expensive card is never dressed up as a powerful one.
  • Moxi won't bluff. The AI answers from real card text and tells you when it doesn't know, instead of making up a ruling.
  • The exact card you own. Scans and imports keep the real printing and finish — not generic stand-ins.
  • Silence over guesses. When the data can't back a claim — an alert, a stat, a delta — the app says nothing rather than making something up.

Questions

Fair questions, straight answers

When does it launch?
Soon, on Google Play first (iOS follows). The honest answer: it launches when it's genuinely ready rather than on a date we'd have to force. Join the Discord to be first in — early community members are how we test and shape it.
Is scanning really free and unlimited?
Yes. Tap-to-scan, unlimited cards, unlimited binders, free forever. Mythic adds convenience and intelligence on top (hands-free auto-scan, the Watcher, cost-to-finish) — it never takes the tracker away.
Does it work offline?
Scanning recognizes cards on your device, so it works with no connection. Prices, legality updates, and Moxi need the internet — they refresh when you're back online.
What makes it different from other collection apps?
Two things. It leads with what you can build — completion % against real archetypes, not just a list of what you own. And it's honest by design: real prices or nothing, playability kept separate from price, and an AI that says "I'm not sure" instead of bluffing.
I've never played Magic. Is this for me?
Genuinely, yes. There's a built-in "I'm new to Magic" path: a five-minute crash course on how the game works, a quiz that finds your playstyle, and Moxi to suggest a first budget deck — no cards needed to start.
Is my collection private?
Your collection lives on your device, with optional account backup so you can restore it on a new phone. We don't sell your data. Prices are clearly labeled third-party estimates. Details in the privacy policy.
What languages does it speak?
Ten: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, and both Simplified and Traditional Chinese — using the official localized Magic terms players see on their printed cards.
