How it works

From shoebox to deck

  1. Scan your cards

    Point your phone at a card and tap. Free, with no limit.

  2. See what you can build

    Your cards are checked against a library of real decks, counting the key cards you own for each.

  3. Finish the deck

    Get the missing cards and what they cost at market prices.

Start from your collection

Decks you can build

Deck Brain starts from the cards you own and works backwards.

  • Decks in and out. Paste a decklist, or with Curator a deck link that keeps the exact printings. Share yours with the community once it passes review.

Why players trust the numbers

Honest by design

No price, no number
A card with no price data shows a dash and stays out of your totals.
Numbers show their base
Completion reads like 12 of 14 key cards, and a win rate over fewer than five games shows a dash.
Filters say what they show
A filtered list reads like 12 of 340 entries.
Proxies held at $0
Scan and build with proxies. They're marked and never counted in your collection's value.
Commander brackets
Checked against Wizards' published limits, with the evidence cards. It gives no bracket number, since the official rubric includes things software can't measure.
Rue says when it isn't sure
Rue reads the exact text of the cards you name and is built to say when it can't confirm a ruling.

You'll see fewer numbers here than in other apps. Each one you do see can be checked.

Scanning

Everyone has the shoebox

The one in the closet you haven't opened in years. Point your phone at it.

  • Read on your phone. After a one-time card list download, tap scanning works with no signal. Prices and exact printings need a connection.
  • Whole stacks.Curator or Attic Pass Turn on AUTO and feed it cards. When it reads a card it isn't sure of, you hear it and see it instead of getting a guess.
  • Cards nothing can read. Tap Identify by photo and the app works it out from the image. Online, with a daily allowance that's larger with Curator.
  • A spreadsheet of cards. Import a CSV free. With Curator or an Attic Pass it also keeps printing, condition, foil and price paid.
  • Scan to a file.Curator With Export file, scans go into a scan session on your phone and never into your collection. The app then writes an import file for TCGplayer, BinderPOS, ManaPool or Crystal Commerce, or a plain CSV, which is also the file it gives for Shopify. Each session is kept for 30 days, so you can export it again.
  • Buying cards.Curator Buy mode prices each card you scan at today's price and shows a cash offer and a store credit offer. You set both rates and a bulk line. A card with no price today gets no offer. Cards you accept go where the scanner is set to put them, with the price paid recorded.

New players

Magic is a lot

Thirty-odd years of cards, dozens of formats and a rules document longer than most novels. This app starts at the beginning.

  • Learn the game. A twelve-part rules crash course, what the colors mean and a guide to seven formats.
  • Play before you play. A practice game against the app with no cards needed, then a coach for your first real game.
  • Your first deck. A playstyle quiz, help picking a first commander, and Rue to suggest a budget deck. Archetypes carry a play-complexity rating.

FAQ

Questions players ask

Where can I get it?
Google Play first, and the iPhone version is in testing. Join the Discord to hear when each one is out.
On Google Play now. The iPhone version is in testing and comes to the App Store later. Join the Discord to hear when it's out.
Is scanning really free and unlimited?
Yes. Tap to scan as many cards as you like, free for good. Curator adds Auto-scan for stacks.
Does it work offline?
Card names are read on your phone, so after a one-time card list download, scanning works offline. Prices, exact printings, Identify by photo and Rue need the internet.
I've never played Magic. Is this for me?
Yes. There's a rules crash course, a playstyle quiz, a practice game and Rue to suggest a first budget deck. No cards needed to start.
Is my collection private?
Your collection is saved on your phone. Every install signs in as an anonymous guest, and a guest's collection, decks, wishlist, game log and value history are backed up to our servers too. Adding an email in Settings, under Account & Backup, is what lets you restore them on another device. A guest account with no collection backup after 30 days, or no change to it in 180 days, can be removed from our servers. Your phone keeps its copy. When you ask Rue something, your question and a summary of your collection go to our AI provider. No ads, and we never sell your data. Details are in the privacy policy.
What languages does it speak?
Ten. English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese.
