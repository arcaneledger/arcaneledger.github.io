Quick answers

Scanning tips

  • Good light beats everything. Avoid hard glare on foils.
  • Tap-to-scan reads the card name instantly; AUTO mode is for stacks — drop a card, let it settle, drop the next.
  • If a duplicate didn't count in AUTO, nudge the card — the scanner re-reads on movement.
  • Wrong printing matched? Open the card → Printings tab and pick yours.

Account & backup

  • Create an account in Settings → Account & Backup to protect your collection.
  • New phone? Sign in and your collection restores automatically.
  • Forgot your password? Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen — the email link opens the app to set a new one.

Subscriptions

  • Mythic is billed by your app store — Google Play on Android, the App Store on iPhone.
  • Manage or cancel — Android: Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions. iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Reinstalled and Mythic looks locked? Tap RESTORE on the upgrade screen.
  • Unsubscribing never deletes your collection.

Found a bug?

Tell us what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead — a screenshot helps a ton. Drop it in the Discord or email us.
