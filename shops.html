For card shops and vendors

Curator Shop runs your card shop from your phone: the counter, your stock and your staff in one place.

One app. Curator Shop is not a separate app. It is the same Arcane Ledger your customers use, on Google Play for Android (the iPhone version comes later), and the subscription opens the shop side for you and your staff.

One price. $24.99 a month or $239.99 a year, with a 14-day free trial the first time you subscribe. Ten staff seats are included, and more are free if you ask. The store shows the final price and the free trial before you pay.

Curator Shop is its own subscription, separate from Curator, the plan for collectors.

What Curator Shop does

  • Buy, sell and trade at the counter. Scan a customer's cards into a sale, a buy or a trade, priced at your own rates as you go.
  • Inventory. Keep your singles and sealed in stock, and load your stock from a file.
  • Store credit. Give and spend store credit, with each regular's balance kept for you.
  • Holds. Hold cards for a customer until they pick them up.
  • Reprice alerts. See which cards in your stock moved in price, and reprice them in one place.
  • End of day. Totals for the whole shop and for each person at the close, ready to export.
  • Staff and managers. Each person works from their own phone, and you choose who joins and who manages.
  • Exports. Export your inventory or the day's buys as a file for TCGplayer, ManaPool or a spreadsheet.

How to start

  1. Get Arcane Ledger on Google Play.
  2. Open the Tools tab and tap Run a card shop? Curator Shop.
  3. Tap START YOUR SHOP, name your shop, then choose your plan. The free trial starts in the store.
  4. Add your staff from the shop's settings. They use the free app on their own phones.
