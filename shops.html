For card shops

Buying, selling or trading at the counter or at a show? Arcane Ledger does the math while you scan.

The shop tools are part of Curator, at the normal Curator price. There is no separate shop plan. Curator starts with a free trial the first time you subscribe. See pricing.

What the shop tools do

  • Buy offers to the cent. Scan a customer's cards and each one is priced at today's price, with two offers, cash and store credit, at the rates you set. Set different rates by card price, your own percent for each condition, and a bulk rate for cheap cards. A card with no price today gets no offer, never a guess.
  • Sell mode. Scan the cards a customer picks out of your case. Each one shows your price, set as a percent of market and rounded the way you like, and sales tax is added for you.
  • Trade mode. Scan their cards and yours, and the app shows who owes what.
  • Receipts and deals. Every buy, sale and trade gets a receipt you can share. The Deals list keeps every deal with each day's totals and profit, and exports to a spreadsheet.
  • Export files. Scan straight into a file and export it in the layout that several store inventory programs import, or as a plain CSV, then import it into the program you already use. The Scanner lists the layouts it writes.

How to start

  1. Get Arcane Ledger on Google Play.
  2. Open the Scanner, open its settings, and go to the SHOP tab.
  3. Under SHOP MODE, pick BUY, SELL or TRADE. On a free account this opens the upgrade screen, where you can start Curator's free trial.
  4. Set your cash and store credit rates, your condition percents and your shop name (it goes at the top of your receipts), then start scanning.
